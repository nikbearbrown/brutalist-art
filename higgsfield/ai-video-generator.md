# AI Video Generator

Source: https://higgsfield.ai/mcp

Higgsfield documents video generation through the MCP workflow and platform feature index. Videos can be generated from prompts, reference images, or both; the MCP FAQ says videos can be up to 15 seconds across multiple cinematic styles.

Related models include Seedance, Kling, Veo, Minimax Hailuo, Sora, WAN, and Cinema Studio.
