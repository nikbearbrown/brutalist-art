# DOODLE-PROMPTS — Midjourney prompts for every missing beat asset

Generated from the unfilled SHOPPING.md slots across `books/` (scan
2026-07-22: 25 missing of 30 listed; `dashboard-that-lied` fully stocked).
Template is the house doodle profile:

    [SUBJECT], single subject, rough hand-drawn black ink doodle --ar 2:3 --profile 3hckybx --hd

**Recommended addition** (kills the garbled lettering — "John mapier" — and
stray signatures the profile sometimes adds): append
` --no text,lettering,signature,watermark` to every prompt. Labels are the
skill's job, never baked into the art.

**The round trip:** MJ PNG → drop into `doodles/png/<name>.png` (the tier-2
library) → Codex vectorizes the keepers into `doodles/svg/<category>/` →
`python3 skills/make/doodle/scripts/build_index.py`. To ALSO fill the reel's
vox slot directly, copy the PNG to the reel's `pantry/<BID>.png` (the vox
treatment desaturates + grains it; already-monochrome doodles ride through
fine — note this restyles that beat from documentary-photo to doodle, which
is the point).

Checkbox = generated & dropped in. ⚠ = phrase-checked against the title
only (device offline during detail pass) — skim the reel's own SHOPPING.md
entry before generating.
