AI renders it. You decide what to build with it. The Brutalist Manim Shelf walks open-source Manim libraries one at a time — every scene rendered and running, no fabrication, no slides.

This video: manim-physics by Matheart. Two animated scenes: a 3D magnetic field from a circular current-carrying wire (camera rotating to show the field structure), and TwoObjectsFalling — a rigid body simulation with pymunk where a circle and rotated square fall under gravity, bounce off walls, and collide. The library wraps pymunk physics and manim's 3D field rendering into a single import.
