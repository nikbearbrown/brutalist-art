What Is Brutalist?

Brutalist is a free, local, audio-first toolkit that turns a folder of your work into a finished explainer video — the beat sheet is the program that renders the film.

Brutalist is open source. Download it, use it, make your own videos:
https://github.com/nikbearbrown/brutalist.art
Self-serve — no support, no email. If you find it useful, a star is appreciated.
