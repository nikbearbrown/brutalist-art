## Purpose

This is the intro episode of the **Brutalist meta-series** — a video about the
toolkit, made by the toolkit. The pedagogical goal is narrower than a full
explainer: the viewer should leave knowing (a) why one-click AI video fails,
(b) what the conductor model is, and (c) what three terminal commands start any
build. It is not a deep-explainer in the beat-mix sense (no vox beats, no YOUR
TURN prompt). That is intentional and acceptable for a meta-series cold-open.
