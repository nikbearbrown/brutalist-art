## Remaining Concerns

1. **Still resolution warning:** All six PNG stills are 1280×720 (source resolution) but the output is 1920×1080. compile.py upscales them with Ken Burns — the warning "will reveal upscale artifacts" was printed during compilation. This is a pre-existing source condition (the stills were generated at 720p). No action taken because (a) the source reel has the same stills at the same resolution, (b) the instruction prohibits replacing supplied media with generated approximations, and (c) upscaling artifacts are minor with Ken Burns motion.

2. **Manim retime:** Several Manim beats are slowed significantly (T03: 1.64×, B09 medhavy: 1.83×). This is within the 3× limit but the slower playback may reveal visible motion slowdown on these equation-tangent beats. The Manim source scenes are owned by the source reel and cannot be re-rendered without the vox_scenes.py Manim pipeline.

3. **Motion histogram:** graphic beats at 44% (medhavy) / 42% (HAI) — slightly over the ~40% cap noted in the compile output. This is inherited from the source reel structure.
