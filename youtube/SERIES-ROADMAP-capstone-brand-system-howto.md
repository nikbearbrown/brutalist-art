# SERIES ROADMAP — "Make It Count: The Complete Brand System (Capstone)"

A how-to ai-explainer series for **INFO 7375 — Complete Brand System** (the final: a live brand
system + a 10-minute presentation, **200 pts**, due August 14, 2026). Audience: **students who
built every piece across A1–A10 and now have to integrate it into one coherent system and present
it live.** Register: **pragmatic, step-by-step** (Liam voice, `claude-liam`, Kokoro am_onyx). The
series walks a beginner from "I have ten assignments' worth of parts" to "one live, coherent brand
system I can present in 9:30 and a tool I can demo in real time."

> Same path you've kept all semester (A personal / B Madison-tool startup). Voice: `claude-liam`;
> `claude-hai` (students channel) is the one-variable alternative.

## The standard (hold it all series)

**"Would this hold up in front of a client?"** That's the bar — not "did I learn," but "can I
demonstrate." This is integration, not creation: everything already exists across A1–A10; the job
is to make it cohere, make it live, and deliver it. And the capstone truth from the brief: *the
real world doesn't wait for polish — what matters is that it's all real, all yours, built in one
semester.*

## Honesty / craft threads (DOUBLE-CHECK LAW) — the capstone versions

- **"Live means live."** The website is hosted and functional on presentation day — no localhost,
  no "still finishing." The tool demo runs in real time, not screenshots. Say it every episode.
- **Demo or die.** Show the tool working live; have a backup (a recorded fallback) in case it
  breaks — the same "backup plan if the demo fails" discipline from the pitch midterm. And the
  money/hard-stop habit still applies: if any live demo step would spend money, that's a human stop.
- **10 minutes is a HARD cap.** Rehearse to **9:30**; points come off for going over. Timing is a
  graded skill, not a suggestion.
- **Coherence is 40 points.** Every element must reinforce the brand — nothing out of place. The
  video teaches auditing the whole system as one, using the A7–8 style guide as the yardstick.
- **The bonus is for doing something nobody else did** — not for completing requirements well.
  Name that honestly so students don't expect +10 for polish.

## Series doctrine (every episode)

- **Assume the parts exist but the system doesn't yet.** Each episode pulls specific A1–A10
  outputs and integrates them. Define every term first time (brand system, touchpoint, coherence,
  live demo, positioning statement, persona). Never "just."
- **One integration or rehearsal deliverable per episode**, with the paste-ready prompt (often a
  house skill — `nina`, `madison-pitch`, `brandy`) that assembles or audits it.
- **ai-explainer bookends stay:** composer cold open → step-by-step body → verdict recap → Your
  Turn → title-restate outro. 16:9. Free pipeline. `"register": "Pragmatist"`.

## The episodes (build in this order)

### E1 — "The Capstone Map: One System from Ten Assignments" (~5 min)
- **slug:** `claude-liam-cap-01-the-map`
- **premise:** You're not making new things — you're proving the things you made are one brand. See
  the whole board and the client-standard first.
- **teach:** the "would this hold up in front of a client?" bar; inventory every deliverable and
  where it came from (website ← A8/A7; identity ← A7; strategy ← A6/A5; social ← A9/A8; stories ←
  A9; tool ← A3/A4/A5B; path-specific ← A8 resumes / pitch deck); the six required core pieces +
  the path-specific bundle; the six-part pro structure (Objective → Audience → Positioning →
  Strategy → Execution → Demo). This is integration, not creation.
- **traps:** treating it as a summary; missing a deliverable; path drift from earlier assignments.
- **deliverable:** the capstone inventory + gap list. Your Turn = a prompt that audits the
  student's A1–A10 outputs against the capstone checklist and flags what's missing.

### E2 — "Make It All Live: Website + Tool Demo Readiness" (~6 min)
- **slug:** `claude-liam-cap-02-make-it-live`
- **premise:** The two things that can sink you on presentation day are a dead URL and a demo that
  won't run. Lock both first.
- **teach:** get the **website hosted and functional** on a real URL (Vercel/Framer/Wix/etc.) —
  "live means live"; the **working tool demo** link or access credentials; rehearse the **live
  demo** (2–3 key features, the methodology behind it) and build a **backup** (recorded fallback)
  in case it breaks. Test both from a device that isn't yours. Demo or die.
- **traps:** localhost; "I'm still finishing it"; a demo that only worked once; no backup.
- **deliverable:** a live website URL + a working, rehearsed demo + a fallback. Your Turn = "open
  your site and your demo on someone else's phone — every failure is a fix before presentation day."

### E3 — "Assemble the Brand System & Audit for Coherence (Consistency, 40 pts)" (~6 min)
- **slug:** `claude-liam-cap-03-assemble-and-audit`
- **premise:** Coherence is 40 of 200 points. Bring every touchpoint together and make sure
  nothing feels out of place.
- **teach:** assemble the **identity system** (logo + a variation, palette with hex, primary/
  secondary type, a 1–2 slide style-guide summary — from A7), the **strategy doc** (UVP, 3–5
  pillars, 2+ personas, competitive positioning — from A6/A5), **3–5 social posts** (from A9/A8),
  **2 brand stories** (from A9), and the **path-specific bundle** (A: two resumes + optimized
  LinkedIn + 3–5 portfolio samples; B: 3-slide pitch deck + optimized LinkedIn + 1–2 page tool
  docs). Then run a **coherence audit** across website / LinkedIn / Substack / social / deck using
  the style guide as the yardstick — same family, nothing off.
- **traps:** disjointed touchpoints; a deck that doesn't match the site; missing path-specific items.
- **deliverable:** the assembled system + a coherence audit. Your Turn = `brandy` (or a prompt)
  that audits the touchpoints against the A7 style guide and flags inconsistencies.

### E4 — "Build the 10-Minute Presentation" (~6 min)
- **slug:** `claude-liam-cap-04-build-the-deck`
- **premise:** Great work presented badly still loses. Build the deck to the exact time budget and
  the pro structure.
- **teach:** the presentation structure with per-section budgets — **Brand Intro (1 min)** (name +
  positioning statement, one-slide brand essence), **Strategy (2 min)** (audience, UVP, pillars,
  competitive positioning), **Visual Identity Showcase (2 min)** (logo variations, palette, type,
  website walkthrough, social examples), **Brand Storytelling (2 min)** (present ONE story in full,
  name the framework and why, connect to values), **Tool Demo (3 min)** (live, 2–3 features, the
  methodology). Slide design carries the brand (this is where the A7 assets + `brutalist-slides` /
  Remotion can make a standout deck). `madison-pitch` for the structure.
- **traps:** unbudgeted sections; reading slides; a story with no named framework; a text-heavy deck.
- **deliverable:** the presentation deck. Your Turn = `madison-pitch` (or a prompt) that structures
  the 10-minute deck to the section budgets from the student's assembled system.

### E5 — "Rehearse to 9:30 + Nail the Live Demo (Presentation, 20 pts + Bonus +10)" (~5 min)
- **slug:** `claude-liam-cap-05-rehearse-and-demo`
- **premise:** The last mile — practice until the timing is automatic and the demo is boring
  because it always works.
- **teach:** rehearse to **9:30** (points off over 10:00 — time yourself, cut mercilessly);
  professional presence (organized, confident, engaging); run the **live demo** cleanly (2–3
  features, methodology, backup ready); and the honest note on the **bonus** — +5–10 is for a bold
  creative risk nobody else took ("demo or die"), NOT for doing the requirements well. Aim your
  one memorable moment.
- **traps:** going over time; a fragile demo; expecting the bonus for competence; underpreparing.
- **deliverable:** a timed, rehearsed run + a reliable demo. Your Turn = "record one full run,
  timed — if you're over 9:30 or the demo stalls, that's tonight's practice."

### E6 — "Ship It: Slides + Live URLs + Demo Access" (~3 min) — closer
- **slug:** `claude-liam-cap-06-submit`
- **premise:** Three things must be in place before you present — no exceptions. Don't lose 200
  points on logistics.
- **teach:** the submission — **slide presentation uploaded** by the due time, **website URL live
  and functional**, **tool demo link or access credentials** working; a final coherence + live
  check of all three from a fresh device. "All three must be in place before you present."
- **deliverable:** all three submitted and verified live. Your Turn = "the night before, open your
  slides, your site, and your demo on a device you've never used for this — if any of the three
  fails, that's your only job tonight."
