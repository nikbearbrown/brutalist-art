# Sources — The Ad That Answered Itself

## Primary source (the artifact under teardown)

**Anthropic — "Can AI be trusted?" (Claude ad).**
YouTube: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jVbGX7zJHi8
Runtime ~1:13. A chain of spoken rhetorical questions over grainy, Polaroid-grain
footage; no narration of authority, no product demo until the logo.

Score: **Duval Timothy — "Ball" (2017)**, Apron Records (credited in-thread to Duval
Timothy and Steven Julian of Apron Records). Recorded ~9 years before the ad.

### Ad copy (verbatim, from the video's on-screen captions)

- "Can AI be trusted?"
- "Who's gonna hit the brakes, if we need to?"
- "How do we really ensure that what we're aiming to achieve really does benefit the majority of people?"
- "If it ends up taking like almost all the jobs, then what does it mean to work?"
- "But wait a minute, why do we have to have this stuff?"
- "If a machine can pretend to care, better than I can actually care, how do we draw the line there?"
- "If we all had a voice in it, then I feel like it would be better."
- "Could AI help people stop feeling misunderstood?"
- "Could AI help me build more connections in the community?"
- "Can AI help me be a better teacher, better mom?"
- "Maybe it'll cure some great things, you know? Things that we're not even at the cusp of understanding yet."
- "Will it create a group of people that ask more questions?"
- "What if we started to be more human again?"
- "We don't want to lose the most beautiful parts of life."
